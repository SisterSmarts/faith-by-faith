Faith By Faith Presents

The Becoming Library

A collection of beautiful guided journals created to help women rediscover who they are, heal with grace, deepen their faith and walk confidently into the lives they were created to live.

“She didn’t become her all at once. She became her one brave choice at a time.”

Explore the Collection

More than pages to complete.

A quiet place to pause, reflect, pray and keep becoming.

Every woman carries a story. Some chapters are filled with joy. Others ask for healing, courage, patience and faith.

The Becoming Library was created to meet women within those chapters—not with pressure to have everything figured out, but with compassionate guidance, honest reflection and room to listen to their hearts.

Each volume feels like sitting down with someone who believes in you. Through heartfelt letters, thoughtful questions, gentle prayers and practical moments of reflection, the collection walks beside you from identity and confidence to healing, freedom, faith, purpose and empowerment.

Becoming Her

Volume 2

Becoming Confident

A reflective journey for replacing self-doubt with a quieter, stronger and more grounded confidence.

Volume 3

Becoming Whole

Healing • Wholeness • Restoration

Volume 4

Becoming Brave

Bravery • Resilience • Bold choices

A guided space for meeting fear honestly and taking the next brave step, even before certainty arrives.

Volume 5

Becoming Free

Freedom • Release • New beginnings

A thoughtful invitation to release what no longer belongs in your story and make room for peace.

Faith • Peace • Spiritual grounding

Becoming Unstoppable

Purpose • Perseverance • Momentum

Empowerment • Leadership • Impact
