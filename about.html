About Faith

Author • Community Builder

My journey has been shaped by faith, learning, reflection, and a deep desire to help others discover their purpose and potential.

I believe growth begins with learning, deepens through reflection, and becomes transformation through action. Through writing, community conversations, and meaningful dialogue, I seek to create spaces where women can grow in confidence, embrace wisdom, and become who they were created to be.

As an author, my work explores confidence, identity, legacy, knowledge, and personal transformation. Whether through books, discussions, workshops, or community-building initiatives, my goal is to encourage people to think deeply, live intentionally, and grow courageously.

Faith By Faith is more than a collection of books and conversations. It is a space for learning, reflection, meaningful conversation, and personal growth—a place where ideas are explored, wisdom is shared, and transformation begins. Here, learning sparks growth, growth inspires purpose, and purpose shapes who we become.

“Learn. Grow. Become.”

Faith Wich Walgwe
