My journey has been shaped by faith, learning, reflection, and a desire to help others discover their purpose.

I believe growth begins with learning, deepens through reflection, and becomes transformation through action.

Through writing, conversations, and community building, I create spaces for women to grow in confidence and clarity.

Impact Through Kiva

Empowering Women. Strengthening Families.

Through our Kiva lending community, we’ve helped fund nearly 300 loans across 33 countries, supporting women building farms, shops, and small businesses.

These loans provide access to inventory, equipment, education, and working capital—helping women create stability and opportunity.

What we do is small in scale, but meaningful in impact: hundreds of women supported through access to opportunity.
