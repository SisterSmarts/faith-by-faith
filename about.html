Impact Through Kiva

Empowering Women. Strengthening Families.

Through our Kiva lending community, we’ve helped fund nearly 300 loans across 33 countries, supporting women who are building farms, running shops, and growing small businesses to support their families.

These loans provide access to inventory, equipment, education, and working capital—helping women turn effort into stability and opportunity.

Our contribution is small in the context of the world’s challenges, but it represents something meaningful: hundreds of opportunities placed in the hands of women investing in themselves, their families, and their futures.
