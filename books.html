Why These Books Were Written

Faith By Faith books come from years of working alongside people, communities, and organisations in different settings, from mentoring programs and women’s initiatives to development work, social entrepreneurship, training, and supporting survivors of human trafficking as they rebuilt their lives.

Through this work, I witnessed how experiences shape the way people see themselves, their choices, and what they believe is possible. For survivors rebuilding their lives after human trafficking, confidence was not simply about feeling better, it was part of finding identity, independence, and a future again. In other situations, I saw people held back by self-doubt, fear, uncertainty, or a lack of belief in their own abilities.

Becoming Confidently You: Live Your Life by the Confidence Code began through my mentoring programs, where I repeatedly saw the same challenges affecting people’s progress. It was originally created as a practical guide for mentors and mentees to continue the conversations beyond the mentoring relationship, before becoming a resource for anyone wanting to strengthen their confidence and understand themselves better.

Knowledge Key came from a different place, a desire to encourage people to question, learn, and think carefully about faith and religion rather than simply accept information without reflection.

These books are not written to offer quick encouragement or easy answers. They are created from real experiences, observations, and questions, with the intention of starting honest conversations and encouraging people to think differently.
