Why Faith By Faith Books Were Created

Faith By Faith resources are born from real conversations, mentoring experiences, and a desire to provide practical guidance for personal growth.

The Confidence Code began through years of mentoring, where I repeatedly saw confidence become one of the biggest barriers preventing people from recognising their own potential.

What started as a resource for mentors and mentees became a guide for anyone wanting to understand themselves, challenge limiting beliefs, and build lasting confidence.

These books are not created to offer temporary encouragement alone. They invite reflection, honesty, self-awareness, and meaningful growth.
