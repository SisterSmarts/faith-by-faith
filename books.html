Why These Books Were Written

Faith By Faith books come from years of working with people, communities, and organisations across different areas, from mentoring programs and women’s initiatives to development work, social entrepreneurship, training, and supporting survivors of human trafficking as they rebuilt their lives. Across these experiences, I repeatedly saw the importance of identity, confidence, knowledge, and understanding. For survivors starting again after unimaginable circumstances, rebuilding confidence was part of rebuilding a life. For others, it appeared through different challenges,  uncertainty, self-doubt, limited opportunities, or the need to find direction.

Becoming Confidently You: Live Your Life by the Confidence Code began through my mentoring work, where I saw the same challenges appearing again and again.

It was first developed as a practical guide for mentors and mentees, then expanded into a resource for anyone wanting to strengthen their confidence and relationship with themselves.

Knowledge Key was created from a different place, a desire to encourage people to ask questions, seek understanding, and engage thoughtfully with topics around faith and religion.

These books are not written to provide quick answers or temporary encouragement. They are created from real experiences and observations, with the aim of encouraging honest reflection, critical thinking, and personal growth.
