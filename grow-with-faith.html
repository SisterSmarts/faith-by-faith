Work With Faith

Whether through inspiring conversations, speaking engagements, relocation guidance, or community impact, my mission is to help people learn, grow, and navigate meaningful life transitions with wisdom, confidence, and hope.

🌍 Moving to Kenya? Start Here.

Thinking about relocating to Kenya or East Africa? Whether you're moving for work, retirement, ministry, family, business, or a fresh start, I provide practical local guidance to help you understand what everyday life is really like before you make the move. Topics include cost of living, neighborhoods, culture, transportation, healthcare, safety, banking, M-Pesa, and settling into your new home. Educational guidance based on lived experience—not immigration or legal advice.

❤️ Community Impact

Giving back is at the heart of Faith By Faith. Through our Kiva lending community and future initiatives, we support women entrepreneurs and families around the world, creating opportunities for economic empowerment and lasting change.

🎤 Speaking & Workshops

Invite Faith to speak at your church, organization, conference, podcast, school, or community event. Topics include leadership, resilience, faith, women’s empowerment, identity, purpose, lifelong learning, and global citizenship.
