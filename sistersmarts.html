Kiva Impact

Empowering Women. Strengthening Families.

Through our Kiva community, we’ve helped fund nearly 300 loans across 33 countries, supporting women who are building farms, shops, and small businesses to support their families.

These loans provide access to inventory, equipment, education, and working capital—helping women create stability and opportunity in their lives.
