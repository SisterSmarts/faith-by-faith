🌍 Moving to Kenya

Thinking about relocating to Kenya or East Africa? I provide practical guidance based on local knowledge and lived experience to help you understand everyday life before making the move. Topics include neighbourhoods, cost of living, healthcare, banking, M-Pesa, transportation, schools, culture, safety, and settling into your new home. This is educational guidance and not immigration or legal advice.

Experience Shared With Purpose

For more than two decades, I've had the privilege of working across Africa and Europe, supporting communities through education, women's empowerment, social entrepreneurship, leadership development, human trafficking prevention, mentoring, and community impact.

Today, my work has evolved into something more intentional. Rather than offering traditional consulting, I focus on sharing knowledge, creating meaningful conversations, developing practical resources, speaking, and helping people navigate important life transitions with greater confidence.

Whether you're considering a move to Kenya, inviting me to speak, looking for educational resources, or exploring a collaboration, my goal is simple: to help people learn, grow, and move forward with purpose.

"Learning sparks growth. Growth inspires purpose. Purpose shapes who we become."
