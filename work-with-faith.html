🌍 Moving to Kenya? Start Here.

Thinking about relocating to Kenya or East Africa? Whether you're moving for work, retirement, ministry, family, business, or a fresh start, I provide practical local guidance to help you understand what everyday life is really like before you make the move. Topics include cost of living, neighborhoods, culture, transportation, healthcare, safety, banking, M-Pesa, and settling into your new home. Educational guidance based on lived experience—not immigration or legal advice.

Why Work With Faith?

For more than two decades, I've worked across Africa and Europe, partnering with non-profit organizations, businesses, educational institutions, international initiatives, and community-led programs.

My experience spans human trafficking prevention, survivor empowerment, women's leadership, social entrepreneurship, STEM mentorship, international development, public speaking, and education.

Today, I bring that experience together through meaningful conversations, practical guidance, educational resources, and carefully selected collaborations that help people move forward with confidence.
