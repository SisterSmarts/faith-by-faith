🌍 Moving to Kenya? Start Here

Thinking about relocating to Kenya or East Africa? Whether you're moving for work, retirement, family, ministry, business or simply looking for a new beginning, I provide practical guidance based on local knowledge and lived experience. Educational guidance based on lived experience. This is not immigration or legal advice.

Guided by Purpose

For more than two decades, I've had the privilege of working across Africa and Europe, partnering with non-profit organisations, businesses, educational institutions, international initiatives, and community-led programmes.

Over the years, I've worked alongside survivors, entrepreneurs, educators, community leaders, and organisations committed to creating lasting change. Those experiences continue to shape how I teach, speak, and support others today.

Today, I share those lessons through books, meaningful conversations, speaking, educational resources, relocation guidance, and carefully selected collaborations—helping people move forward with confidence and purpose.
